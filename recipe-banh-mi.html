Quick Chicken Banh Mi

A crisp baguette packed with savoury chicken, pickles, herbs, and chilli mayo.

Total time25 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix carrot with vinegar and a pinch each of sugar and salt; leave 10 minutes.

  2. Coat chicken with soy sauce. Cook in a hot lightly oiled pan for 6–7 minutes until no pink remains.

  3. Mix mayonnaise and chilli sauce.

  4. Split and lightly toast baguettes.

  5. Spread with chilli mayo and fill with chicken, drained carrot, cucumber, and coriander.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Drain the quick pickle well or it will make the bread soggy.
