Beef Bulgogi

Caramelised marinated beef with sweet pear, soy, and sesame.

Total time25 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix soy sauce, pear, sugar, sesame oil, and garlic.

  2. Add beef and onion; mix and leave for 10 minutes.

  3. Heat a wide pan on high until very hot.

  4. Cook beef in two batches, 2–3 minutes each, letting the edges brown.

  5. Serve immediately over rice.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Freeze the beef for 20 minutes first—it becomes much easier to slice thinly.
