Beef Chow Mein

Slippery noodles, crisp vegetables, and juicy beef in a glossy sauce.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, and 2 tbsp water.

  2. Prepare noodles according to the packet, drain, and rinse briefly.

  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil on high. Cook beef in one layer for 90 seconds per side; remove.

  4. Add remaining oil, cabbage, and carrot. Stir-fry for 2 minutes.

  5. Add noodles, beef, and sauce. Toss with two utensils for 2 minutes until glossy.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Slice beef across the grain; the short fibres make every bite tender.
