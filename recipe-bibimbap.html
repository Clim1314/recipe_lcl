Korean · Easy

Weeknight Bibimbap

Colourful rice bowls topped with vegetables, beef, egg, and spicy sauce.

Total time35 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix gochujang with 1 tbsp water and 1 tsp sesame oil.

  2. Cook carrot, zucchini, and spinach separately in a lightly oiled pan, 2–3 minutes each.

  3. Cook beef with soy sauce for 5 minutes until browned and no pink remains.

  4. Fry eggs until whites are set.

  5. Divide rice into bowls. Arrange vegetables and beef in sections, top with egg and sauce, then mix before eating.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Cook each vegetable separately so the colours stay bright and nothing turns watery.
