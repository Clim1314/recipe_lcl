Easy Butter Chicken

Creamy tomato chicken with gentle spice and a rich restaurant-style sauce.

Total time40 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Toss chicken with curry powder and half the salt.

  2. Melt 1 tbsp butter in a pan on medium-high. Cook chicken for 6 minutes; remove.

  3. Add remaining butter and garlic. Stir for 30 seconds.

  4. Add tomatoes and garam masala. Simmer uncovered for 10 minutes.

  5. Return chicken and simmer 5 minutes until cooked through.

  6. Turn heat low, stir in cream, taste for salt, and warm without boiling.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Add cream only on low heat so the sauce stays smooth.
