Easy Hokkien Mee

Thick noodles tossed with prawns and greens in a dark savoury sauce.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix both soy sauces, oyster sauce, sugar, and 3 tbsp water.

  2. Rinse noodles briefly under warm water and loosen them.

  3. Heat oil on high. Cook prawns for 2 minutes; remove.

  4. Add garlic and bok choy stems; stir-fry 1 minute.

  5. Add noodles, sauce, leafy greens, and prawns. Toss for 3 minutes until glossy and hot.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Loosen noodles before they enter the pan; forcing a solid block will tear them.
