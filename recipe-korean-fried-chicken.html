Korean Crispy Chicken

Crunchy chicken bites coated in a sweet, spicy, sticky glaze.

Total time40 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Pat chicken very dry, then toss with cornflour and salt.

  2. Mix gochujang, honey, soy sauce, vinegar, and 2 tbsp water.

  3. Heat oil in a wide pan on medium-high. Cook chicken in one layer for 5 minutes per side.

  4. Check the thickest piece has no pink centre, then move chicken to a plate.

  5. Wipe the pan carefully. Simmer sauce for 1 minute, add chicken, and toss until coated.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Crowding traps steam. Cook in two batches if the pieces cannot sit in one layer.
