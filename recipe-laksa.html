Easy Coconut Laksa

Creamy, spicy coconut noodle soup with chicken and prawns.

Total time35 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Prepare noodles according to the packet and divide between bowls.

  2. Cook curry paste in a pot with 1 tbsp oil for 1 minute.

  3. Add coconut milk and stock slowly while stirring. Bring to a gentle simmer.

  4. Add chicken and simmer 7 minutes.

  5. Add prawns and cook 3 minutes until opaque and curled.

  6. Stir in fish sauce. Pour over noodles and top with sprouts and lime.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Once coconut milk is added, use a gentle simmer rather than a violent boil.
