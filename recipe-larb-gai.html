Larb Gai

A lively minced chicken salad with lime, herbs, chilli, and toasted rice.

Total time25 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Put chicken and 2 tbsp water in a pan on medium heat.

  2. Stir and break up chicken for 6–7 minutes until fully cooked.

  3. Turn off heat and let it cool for 2 minutes.

  4. Stir in lime juice, fish sauce, sugar, onion, and chilli.

  5. Fold in mint and toasted rice powder just before serving.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Dress the chicken while warm, not scorching hot, so the herbs stay fresh.
