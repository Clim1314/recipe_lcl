Mango Sticky Rice

Sweet coconut sticky rice with ripe mango and a lightly salted sauce.

Total time40 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Soak rice in plenty of water for at least 4 hours, then drain.

  2. Steam rice in a cloth-lined steamer for 25 minutes until translucent and chewy.

  3. Warm 200 ml coconut milk with sugar and half the salt until dissolved; do not boil.

  4. Pour over hot rice, cover, and rest 20 minutes.

  5. Warm remaining coconut milk with remaining salt and cornflour until slightly thick.

  6. Serve rice with sliced mango, coconut sauce, and sesame seeds.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

This requires glutinous rice; ordinary jasmine rice will not have the right texture.
