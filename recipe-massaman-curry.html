Easy Massaman Curry

A mild, warmly spiced coconut curry with chicken, potato, and peanuts.

Total time50 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Cook curry paste with 3 tbsp coconut milk in a pot for 1 minute.

  2. Add chicken and stir for 3 minutes.

  3. Add remaining coconut milk, potatoes, onion, and 150 ml water.

  4. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook 25 minutes.

  5. Check potatoes with a knife; they should be soft. Stir in fish sauce and sugar.

  6. Top with peanuts and serve.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Keep potato pieces the same size so they finish cooking together.
