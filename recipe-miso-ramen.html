Easy Miso Ramen

A creamy, savoury noodle bowl made with supermarket ingredients.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Boil eggs for 7 minutes, cool in cold water, peel, and halve.

  2. Bring stock to a gentle simmer. Whisk miso with a ladle of hot stock in a small bowl.

  3. Pour the miso mixture back in. Add soy sauce and sesame oil; keep below a hard boil.

  4. Cook noodles separately according to the packet and divide between bowls.

  5. Pour over broth and top with egg, corn, and spring onions.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Never boil miso hard; gentle heat protects its smooth flavour.
