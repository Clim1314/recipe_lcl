Nasi Goreng

Smoky Indonesian fried rice with sweet soy and a crispy fried egg.

Total time25 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix kecap manis and soy sauce. Break up cold rice.

  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil on high. Cook chicken for 5 minutes until done.

  3. Add garlic and cabbage; stir-fry 1 minute.

  4. Add rice and sauce. Toss for 3 minutes until hot and evenly brown.

  5. In another pan, fry eggs in remaining oil until edges are crisp.

  6. Serve rice topped with an egg.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Kecap manis is thick sweet soy sauce; regular soy is not a direct substitute.
