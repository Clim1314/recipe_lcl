Foolproof Pad Thai

Tangy, savoury rice noodles with prawns, egg, peanuts, and lime.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Soak noodles in room-temperature water until bendable but still firm; drain.

  2. Mix fish sauce, sugar, lime juice, and 3 tbsp water.

  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil on medium-high. Cook prawns for 2–3 minutes; remove.

  4. Add remaining oil and eggs. Scramble for 30 seconds.

  5. Add noodles and sauce. Toss for 3–4 minutes until noodles are tender.

  6. Return prawns, add sprouts, and toss 30 seconds. Top with peanuts.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Do not boil the noodles first; soaking keeps them from becoming mushy in the pan.
