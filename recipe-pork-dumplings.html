Crispy Pork Dumplings

Juicy dumplings with crisp golden bottoms and a foolproof steam-fry method.

Total time40 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix pork, cabbage, spring onions, soy sauce, and sesame oil until sticky.

  2. Put 1 heaped tsp filling in each wrapper. Wet the edge, fold, and pinch firmly closed.

  3. Heat oil in a non-stick pan on medium. Arrange dumplings flat-side down and cook 2 minutes.

  4. Add water and immediately cover. Steam for 6 minutes.

  5. Remove lid and cook 2–3 minutes until the water is gone and bottoms are crisp.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Do not overfill the wrappers; a tight seal matters more than fancy pleats.
