Salmon Sushi Bowl

All the fresh flavours of sushi with none of the rolling.

Total time20 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Use salmon sold as safe for raw eating, or cook it fully in a pan for 5 minutes.

  2. Mix soy sauce and sesame oil; coat the salmon.

  3. Stir rice vinegar through warm rice and divide between bowls.

  4. Arrange salmon, cucumber, and avocado on top.

  5. Finish with nori and any remaining sauce.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

If the fish label does not clearly say it is suitable for raw consumption, cook it.
