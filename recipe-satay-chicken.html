Chicken Satay

Charred chicken skewers with a creamy, tangy peanut sauce.

Total time35 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. If using wooden skewers, soak them in water for 20 minutes.

  2. Mix chicken with soy sauce, curry powder, honey, and oil.

  3. Thread chicken loosely onto skewers.

  4. Cook in a hot grill pan for 4–5 minutes per side until cooked through.

  5. Warm peanut butter and coconut milk in a small pan, stirring smooth. Add lime juice.

  6. Serve skewers with sauce on the side.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Leave tiny gaps between chicken pieces so heat reaches every surface.
