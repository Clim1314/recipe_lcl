Singapore Noodles

Bouncy rice noodles with curry, prawns, egg, and crisp vegetables.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Soak noodles according to the packet, drain well, and cut twice with scissors.

  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wok. Scramble eggs until just set; remove.

  3. Add remaining oil and prawns. Cook 2–3 minutes; remove.

  4. Stir-fry pepper and carrot for 2 minutes. Add curry powder and stir 20 seconds.

  5. Add noodles, soy sauce, egg, and prawns. Toss for 2 minutes until evenly yellow and hot.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Drain the noodles thoroughly; excess water dilutes the curry flavour.
