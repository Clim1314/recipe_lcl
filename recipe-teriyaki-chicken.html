Teriyaki Chicken

Sticky-sweet glazed chicken with a deeply savoury finish.

Total time25 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Mix soy sauce, mirin, sugar, and ginger until the sugar dissolves.

  2. Pat chicken dry. Heat oil in a pan on medium-high and place chicken smooth-side down.

  3. Cook 5 minutes, turn, and cook another 4 minutes until cooked through.

  4. Lower heat. Pour in sauce and turn chicken repeatedly for 2–3 minutes until glossy.

  5. Slice chicken and spoon the pan sauce over rice. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Lower the heat before adding sugar-rich sauce so it thickens without burning.
