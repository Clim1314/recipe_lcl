Thai Green Curry

A fragrant coconut curry with tender chicken and crisp vegetables.

Total time35 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Spoon the thick coconut cream from the top of the can into a pan; cook 2 minutes.

  2. Add curry paste and stir for 1 minute until fragrant.

  3. Add chicken and stir for 3 minutes.

  4. Pour in remaining coconut milk. Simmer gently for 10 minutes.

  5. Add pepper and beans; simmer 5 minutes.

  6. Season with fish sauce and sugar, then stir in basil off the heat.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Curry pastes vary in heat. Start with less and add more after tasting.
