Tom Yum Goong

Bright hot-and-sour prawn soup with lemongrass, lime, and chilli.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Bring stock, lemongrass, and galangal to a gentle boil for 10 minutes.

  2. Add mushrooms and simmer 4 minutes.

  3. Add prawns and cook 2–3 minutes until pink and opaque.

  4. Turn off heat. Stir in fish sauce, lime juice, and chilli paste.

  5. Taste: add lime for sourness, fish sauce for salt, or chilli for heat.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Add lime juice after turning off the heat so it stays fresh and bright.
