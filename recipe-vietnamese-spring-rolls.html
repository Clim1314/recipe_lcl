Fresh Spring Rolls

Cool, crunchy rice-paper rolls with prawns, noodles, and herbs.

Total time30 minServes2DifficultyEasy

Get ready

Ingredients

For 2 generous servings

Let’s cook

Step by step

  1. Prepare vermicelli according to the packet, rinse cold, and drain very well.

  2. Fill a wide shallow dish with warm—not hot—water.

  3. Dip one wrapper for 5 seconds; it should still feel slightly firm.

  4. Place fillings across the lower third, leaving a border.

  5. Fold the bottom over filling, fold sides inward, then roll tightly.

  6. Repeat one at a time and keep rolls separated.

Read this firstIdiot-proof tip

Rice paper keeps softening after dipping. Remove it while it still feels slightly firm.
